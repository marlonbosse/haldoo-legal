Delete your account and data

Last updated: 8 October 2026

On the web

Sign in to the Haldoo web app and open the "Delete account" page (/account/delete).

By email

Write to mobdev@tuta.com (subject "Haldoo deletion") from the email address of your account. We delete your data within 30 days. The same applies to a beta registration without an account.

What is not removed automatically

  • The local database on your device: uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • A Google Play purchase is not refunded by deleting your account (there are currently no purchases).
