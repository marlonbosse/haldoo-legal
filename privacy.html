What the app does

Haldoo combines a to-do list and habit tracker with Android reminders. The app contains no ads and no tracking SDKs.

Local mode (no account)

Tasks, notes, habits, check-ins, settings and reminder times stay on your device. We receive nothing. The app opts out of Android backups; uninstalling or clearing app data removes everything.

Account and sync (optional)

If you link an account, we store on a server operated by the provider: email address, login data (passwords only as hashes), linked devices, tasks, notes, habits, check-ins and progress data (points, streaks, rewards). Purpose: the sync with the web app and your other devices that you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers use HTTPS.

Push notifications

For sync hints the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), which processes technical device identifiers. The web app can optionally set up web push with your browser. Reminders scheduled locally on the device need no server.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code. Detection runs on the device (Google ML Kit); images are neither stored nor transmitted.

Optional usage analytics

Off by default and requires a linked account. With your consent we record account ID, time, platform and predefined screen/action names, never task, note or habit contents. Deleted within 90 days at the latest; turning it off deletes previous events (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Beta registration

Only with explicit consent we store email, language, time and consent version for at most 90 days. Withdraw any time by email.

Purchases

All features are currently free; there is no purchase. If paid features are offered through Google Play later, we will process purchase records to unlock them and update this policy.

Processors and recipients

  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Services, Google Play)

Deletion and your rights

Delete your account and server-side data on the account deletion page. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may complain to a supervisory authority (Lower Saxony: LfD Niedersachsen).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If processing changes, we update this page and its date.
