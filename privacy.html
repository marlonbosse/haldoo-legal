What the app does

Haldoo combines a to-do list and habit tracker with Android reminders. The app contains no ads and no tracking SDKs.

No accounts, no server transfer

Haldoo works entirely locally. Tasks, notes, habits, check-ins, settings, progress data and reminder times stay on your device only. The app creates no accounts, sends no content to a server of the provider and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We receive no personal data from you. The app opts out of Android backups; uninstalling or clearing app data removes everything.

Reminders and permissions

Notifications and reminders are scheduled locally on the device (notification permission, restore after reboot). No camera or location permission is used.

Google Play

Installation and updates go through Google Play, where Google processes data under its own privacy policy. We have no influence on that.

Purchases

All features are currently free. If paid features or an account are offered later, we will update this policy beforehand and point it out in the app.

Your rights

Because we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or hand out. Questions: mobdev@tuta.com. You may complain to a supervisory authority (Lower Saxony: LfD Niedersachsen).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If processing changes, we update this page and its date.
